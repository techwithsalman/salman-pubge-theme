Behind a cedar door two minutes from Dar el Bacha, a nineteenth-century family house has been restored by local artisans: zellige by hand, plaster carved in place, gardens planted with bitter orange and jasmine.

The table

Breakfast in the courtyard, tagine on the roof.

Our cook Fatima Zahra prepares a seasonal menu from the morning market: msemen with amlou at breakfast, slow-cooked tagines at dusk, and mint tea poured from on high whenever you sit down.

  • Breakfast · 08:00 – 11:00
