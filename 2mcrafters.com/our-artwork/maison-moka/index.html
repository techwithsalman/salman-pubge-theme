Our story

A townhouse, a roaster and a stubborn love for mornings.A townhouse, a roaster and a stubborn love for mornings.

Maison Moka began with a two-kilo roaster in a Tanger garage and a promise to treat coffee like bread: slowly, by hand, every day. Today our kitchen bakes before sunrise and roasts every Tuesday, sourcing beans from three partner farms.
