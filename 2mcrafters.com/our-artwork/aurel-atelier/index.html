La maison

Three generations, one quiet obsession.Three generations, one quiet obsession.

Aurel began in 1962 as a two-bench workshop in the Fès medina. Today our goldsmiths still pierce filigree by hand and finish every clasp under a loupe, while our Casablanca studio designs pieces meant to be worn every day and kept for decades.

“Jewellery is the only luxury that becomes more yours each year.”

Salma Aurel, creative director (fictional)
